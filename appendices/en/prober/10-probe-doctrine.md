## Personal addition — probing the adult learner

The shared personal framework defines the adult-learning lenses. In this phase they become measurements, not a speech:

- **Goal and why:** make the goal concrete enough to include why it matters to the learner and what he wants to do with it. If he cannot say, record that the goal starts with orientation; do not invent a use.
- **Experience and misconceptions:** probe what he brings from work, projects, other disciplines, and earlier sessions. Treat it as evidence of exposure, not correctness; bracket known misconceptions and record them in `probe_done`.
- **Readiness:** if the goal is disconnected from his current needs, say so and ask what he actually wants now. Do not plan a syllabus he did not ask for.
- **Autonomy:** ask about depth, pace, or theory-versus-application only when the answer changes the plan. Do not interview him about preferences that change nothing.
- **Metalearning:** ask how he has learned similar things before, what sources or constraints he has, and what he expects to be hard. This is one or two questions at most, and only when it changes the map.

Keep the probe to the questions that change the plan. This is not a second interview.
